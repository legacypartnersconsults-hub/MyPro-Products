Your subscription

MyPro Ready Hub starts with a 7-day free trial, then renews automatically ($8.99 per month or $64.99 per year) until you cancel. Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple (iPhone) or Google Play (Android), not by us.

  • Cancel or change plan on iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → MyPro Ready Hub.
  • Cancel or change plan on Android: Google Play → profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • Restore a purchase on a new phone: sign in, then tap Restore Purchases on the plan screen or in Profile.
  • Refunds: request them from Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play; we cannot issue refunds for store purchases.

Your account and data

You can delete your account and all of its data in the app under Profile → Delete Account, or at https://myproproducts.com/readyhub/delete-account. Our privacy practices are described in the Privacy Policy at https://myproproducts.com/readyhub/privacy.

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription; cancel it separately in the store as described above.

In an emergency

MyPro Ready Hub is a preparedness tool and is not a substitute for emergency services. If you are in danger or need urgent medical help, call 911.
