Delete your MyPro Ready Hub account

MyPro Products LLC · Effective October 5, 2026

You can delete your MyPro Ready Hub account and the data in it at any time.

Option 1: delete it in the app (immediate)

  • Open MyPro Ready Hub and sign in.
  • Go to Profile, then tap Delete Account.
  • Confirm. Your account and data are deleted right away and permanently.

Option 2: request deletion by email

If you can't open the app (for example you uninstalled it, lost your phone or can't sign in), email jruland@myproproducts.com from the email address you registered with, using the subject "Ready Hub Data Deletion Request". Include your name and the email address on the account. We will confirm the request with you and complete it within 30 days. You can also call (833) 369-7762.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in and account.
  • Your profile and home address.
  • Household and health information: medical profile, prescriptions, emergency contacts, and adult, child, pet and special-needs profiles.
  • Your home inventory and insurance policy details.
  • Documents and photos you uploaded or scanned (prescription labels, insurance policy pages, vaccination records, high-value items, exterior photos) and any other files stored for your account.
  • Your saved location and push-notification token.
  • Your checklists, supplies, MyReadyScore history and notification settings.
  • Subscription and promo or referral records tied to your account.

What we may keep, and for how long

Backups made by our hosting provider and short-lived server logs may continue to exist briefly until they are overwritten on the provider's normal schedule. We keep nothing else tied to your account.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it separately in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. Apple and Google keep their own purchase records under their own policies.
