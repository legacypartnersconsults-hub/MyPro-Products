MyPro Ready Hub Privacy Policy

MyPro Products LLC · Effective October 5, 2026

This policy explains what information MyPro Ready Hub ("Ready Hub", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it. Ready Hub is operated by MyPro Products LLC and includes the iPhone and Android apps and the web app at ready.myproproducts.com.

Information we collect

Account: your name, email address and password (the password is stored in hashed form by our sign-in provider — we cannot read it), plus your home address and ZIP code.

Household and health details you choose to enter: your allergies, medical conditions and physician; prescriptions (names, dosage, refill dates); emergency contacts; and profiles for other adults, children (including date of birth), pets and household members with special needs, including care notes. All of this is optional.

Home inventory and documents: photos and video you capture to scan a room, photos of prescription labels, documents you add (such as insurance policies, IDs and vaccination records), high-value item photos with serial numbers and values, insurance policy details, and dated photos of your home's exterior.

Location: your ZIP code (which we convert to approximate coordinates) and, only if you choose "Current Location", your device's precise location at the time you use the app. We keep your most recent location so we can show local conditions and send alerts. We do not track your location continuously in the background.

Contacts: if you tap "Import from contacts", the app reads only the single contact you pick (name, phone, email) to fill in an emergency contact. We never upload your address book.

Notifications: your device's push-notification token and your notification preferences.

Subscription and referral information: your plan, trial or subscription status and its start, renewal and end dates, the price charged, the store's transaction identifier, and any promo or referral code you enter. Payments are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play; we never receive your card number. When you subscribe, restore a purchase or open the app, our server confirms your subscription directly with Apple or Google and records the result.

Technical information: our servers keep standard logs (such as IP address, request time and errors) for security and troubleshooting. Settings such as your vault lock stay on your device. We do not currently use third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs, and we do not use advertising identifiers or tracking cookies.

How we use it

We use your information only to run the features you use:

  • Local risk scoring, weather radar, shelters, evacuation routes and hazard alerts for your area.
  • Checklists, supplies, your MyReadyScore, and reminders.
  • Home inventory valuation, document and prescription records, and insurance summaries.
  • ReadyRanger Mason, the preparedness advisor, which answers your questions using the information in your account.
  • Sending push notifications, including weather and hazard alerts near your saved location and a weekly readiness reminder (you can turn these off).
  • Running your account and subscription, crediting promo or referral codes, keeping the service secure, providing support, and meeting legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not build advertising profiles.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers that make Ready Hub work, and only as needed:

  • Supabase — our database, sign-in, file storage and server functions. All account data and files you add are stored there.
  • Google (Gemini and Cloud Vision) — when you scan a room, a prescription label, a document or an insurance policy, the photos or video frames are sent to Google's AI services to read the text and identify items. When you ask Mason a question, the question is sent to Google's Gemini together with a summary of the information in your account, which can include your health details, prescriptions, family members' names and dates of birth, pets, insurance policy details, and your saved location, so Mason can answer. The app asks for your permission before anything is sent to Google's AI services, and you can withdraw it at any time in Profile → Privacy; if you don't allow it, you can still add items and records by hand. We use Google's paid API services, not the free tiers. Under Google's terms for those paid services, Google does not use the content we send to train or improve its products or models, and keeps only limited logs, for a limited time, to detect abuse.
  • Google Maps Platform — address suggestions, geocoding, and route and place lookups. These requests include the address text, ZIP code or coordinates being looked up.
  • Apple Push Notification service and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — to deliver push notifications to your device.
  • Weather, hazard and map sources — the National Weather Service, USGS, FEMA, RainViewer (radar), Esri (map tiles), Open-Meteo, OpenStreetMap (Nominatim and Overpass) and Zippopotam. Requests to them include the ZIP code or coordinates needed to return local results, and they can see your IP address.
  • Google Fonts and Unsplash — the app loads fonts and a welcome-screen image from these services, which can see your IP address.
  • Apple and Google — they process purchases and subscriptions in their stores under their own privacy policies. Our server also sends them your subscription's transaction identifier to confirm its status, and they notify our server when a subscription renews, is cancelled or is refunded.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts the web version of the app.

Emergency messages (SOS, Safe and broadcast) are composed in your phone's own messaging app and sent by you to the contacts you choose; we do not send or see them. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect safety or rights, or in connection with a sale or merger of the business. A promo or referral code's owner is credited when you use their code but does not receive your personal information.

Device permissions

The app asks permission before using the camera (room, prescription and document scanning), photo library (attaching existing photos and files), contacts (importing one emergency contact), location (local conditions and alerts), notifications, and Face ID or Touch ID (the optional vault lock — your device checks your biometrics; we never receive them). You can change any of these at any time in your device settings. Ready Hub does not require notifications or location to be turned on to use the app.

How we protect it

Data is encrypted in transit using TLS and is encrypted at rest by our hosting provider. Our servers return an account's data only to that signed-in account. On iPhone and Android, the app also keeps an offline copy of your data in an encrypted database on your device, with the key held in the device's secure storage. You can turn on a vault lock with Face ID, Touch ID or a PIN. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

How long we keep it, and deleting your account

We keep your information while your account is open. You can delete your account at any time in the app under Profile → Delete Account. Deletion is immediate and permanent: it removes your sign-in, profile, household and health records, inventory, documents, prescription, insurance and exterior photos, scanned files, push token, saved location, and subscription and referral records.

If you cannot open the app, you can request deletion at https://myproproducts.com/readyhub/delete-account or by emailing jruland@myproproducts.com; we will complete verified requests within 30 days. Backups kept by our hosting provider and short-lived server logs may continue to exist briefly until they are overwritten on the provider's normal schedule, and Apple and Google keep their own purchase records.

Room-scan video and photos are used only to analyze the room and are not kept; we save the list of items the scan identified.

Your choices

You can review and edit your profile and household information in the app, turn off notifications or location in your device settings, and delete your account as described above. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights over your personal information, such as access or correction; contact us to exercise them.

Children and family information

Ready Hub is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. A parent or guardian may enter information about their own children (for example, medication details); that information is used only to provide the features you choose.

Changes to this policy

We will post any changes here and update the date above. If a change materially affects how your information is used or shared, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
