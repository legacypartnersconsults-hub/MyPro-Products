Your subscription

MyPro Claim Assist starts with a 7-day free trial, then renews automatically ($9.99 per month or $89.99 per year) until you cancel. Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple (iPhone) or Google Play (Android), not by us.

  • Cancel or change plan on iPhone: Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions, then MyPro Claim Assist.
  • Cancel or change plan on Android: Google Play, then your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions.
  • Restore a purchase on a new phone: sign in, then tap Restore purchases on the plan screen.
  • Refunds: request them from Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play; we cannot issue refunds for store purchases.

Your account and data

You can delete your account and all of its data in the app under Account, then Delete account, or at https://myproproducts.com/claim-assist/delete-account. Our privacy practices are described in the Privacy Policy at https://myproproducts.com/claim-assist/privacy.

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription; cancel it separately in the store as described above.

About the app's guidance

MyPro Claim Assist gives general information. It is not legal advice and is not a licensed public adjuster. For advice about your situation, consult a licensed attorney or public adjuster in your state.
