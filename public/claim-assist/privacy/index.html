MyPro Claim Assist Privacy Policy

MyPro Products LLC · Effective November 6, 2026

This policy explains what information MyPro Claim Assist ("Claim Assist", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it. Claim Assist is operated by MyPro Products LLC and includes the iPhone and Android apps.

Information we collect

Account: your name, email address and password (the password is stored in hashed form by our sign-in provider, and we cannot read it). You can also add a mobile phone number.

Claim information you enter: each claim's property address, insurance company, policy and claim numbers, status, your adjuster's name and contact details, timeline entries, notes, damage descriptions, contractors and their estimates, expenses and reimbursements, policy limits and deductibles, and your checklist progress.

Photos and documents: damage photos, receipts, letters, estimates, adjuster reports and insurance policy documents you take or upload.

Conversations with Mason, the app's AI claim assistant, and the reports it creates for you, plus a daily count of how many AI requests your account has used.

Subscription and referral information: your plan, trial or subscription status and its start, renewal and end dates, the price charged, the store's transaction identifier, and any promo code you enter. Payments are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play, and we never receive your card number. Our server confirms your subscription directly with Apple or Google and records the result.

Technical information: our servers keep standard logs (such as IP address, request time and errors) for security and troubleshooting. We do not use third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools, and we do not use advertising identifiers or tracking cookies. The app does not use your device's location.

How we use it

We use your information only to run the features you use:

  • Storing and organizing your claims, photos, documents, expenses, contractors and timeline.
  • Mason and its features: Claim Coach, Policy Analyzer, Coverage Detector, Settlement Assistant, Photo Checker and Contents Scanner. The Claim Health Score is worked out on our side from your claim's details, without AI.
  • Running your account and subscription, crediting promo codes, keeping the service secure, providing support, and meeting legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not build advertising profiles.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers that make Claim Assist work, and only as needed:

  • Supabase: our database, sign-in, file storage and server functions. All account data and files you add are stored there.
  • Anthropic (the Claude API): when you use Mason or one of its features, your question and the parts of your claim that feature needs (for example claim details, policy text, estimates, expenses or photos) are sent to Anthropic so Mason can answer. The app asks your permission before anything is sent to Anthropic. We use Anthropic's paid commercial API. Under its terms, Anthropic does not use the content we send to train its models, and keeps it only for a limited time.
  • Apple and Google: they process purchases and subscriptions in their stores under their own privacy policies. Our server sends them your subscription's transaction identifier to confirm its status, and they tell our server when a subscription renews, is cancelled or is refunded.

When you share a contents list or open a link to another site (such as MyPro Market), you choose where it goes. A promo code's owner is credited when you use their code but does not receive your personal information. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect safety or rights, or in connection with a sale or merger of the business.

Device permissions

The app asks permission before using the camera (damage, receipt and document photos) and your photo library (attaching photos you already have). You can change these at any time in your device settings.

How we protect it

Data is encrypted in transit using TLS and is encrypted at rest by our hosting provider. Your photos and documents are kept in private storage that only your signed-in account can open, and links to them expire after one hour. Your sign-in is stored in your phone's secure storage (the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore). No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

How long we keep it, and deleting your account

We keep your information while your account is open. You can delete your account at any time in the app under Account, then Delete account. Deletion is immediate and permanent. It removes your sign-in, profile, claims, photos, documents, Mason conversations and reports, and subscription and referral records.

If you cannot open the app, you can request deletion at https://myproproducts.com/claim-assist/delete-account or by emailing support@myproproducts.com. We will complete verified requests within 30 days. Backups kept by our hosting provider and short-lived server logs may continue to exist briefly until they are overwritten on the provider's normal schedule, and Apple and Google keep their own purchase records.

Your choices

You can review and edit your information in the app, change device permissions in your device settings, and delete your account as described above. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights over your personal information, such as access or correction. Contact us to exercise them.

Children

Claim Assist is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We will post any changes here and update the date above. If a change materially affects how your information is used or shared, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
