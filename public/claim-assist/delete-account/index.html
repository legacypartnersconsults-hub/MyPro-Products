Delete your MyPro Claim Assist account

MyPro Products LLC · Effective November 6, 2026

You can delete your MyPro Claim Assist account and the data in it at any time.

Option 1: delete it in the app (immediate)

  • Open MyPro Claim Assist and sign in.
  • Go to Account, then tap Delete account.
  • Confirm. Your account and data are deleted right away and permanently.

Option 2: request deletion by email

If you can't open the app (for example you uninstalled it, lost your phone or can't sign in), email support@myproproducts.com from the email address you registered with, using the subject "Claim Assist Data Deletion Request". Include your name and the email address on the account. We will confirm the request with you and complete it within 30 days. You can also call (833) 369-7762.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in and account, and your name and phone number.
  • All of your claims, including property addresses, adjuster details, timeline, damage items, contractors, expenses, policy details and checklist progress.
  • Photos and documents you uploaded, and any other files stored for your account.
  • Your conversations with Mason, AI reports and AI usage counts.
  • Subscription and promo or referral records tied to your account.

What we may keep, and for how long

Backups made by our hosting provider and short-lived server logs may continue to exist briefly until they are overwritten on the provider's normal schedule. We keep nothing else tied to your account.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it separately in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. Apple and Google keep their own purchase records under their own policies.
